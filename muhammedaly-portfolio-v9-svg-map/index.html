I'm Muhammedaly. Somewhere between a camera, a blank canvas, and a line of code, I found my way of turning ideas into something real.

THE JOURNEY 01WHERE IT BEGAN 02FIRST STEPS 03STARTING EARLY 04A NEW COUNTRY 05CREATING TODAY 06UNWRITTEN SCROLL TO EXPLORE  
2006
Ashgabat, Turkmenistan.

I was born in Ashgabat, Turkmenistan, in 2006. There is a deeply personal part of my beginning: I was a twin, but my twin brother passed away before birth. He will always be part of my story.

2013 · AGE 7
Small steps.
Big beginnings.

At seven years old, I started school. I didn't know where life would take me yet, but those early years were the beginning of a lifelong curiosity to learn and explore.

2019 · AGE 13
13 years old.
Already creating.

At 13, I started working at Ynamdar Online Market, then the number-one online marketplace in Turkmenistan, as a graphic designer and site manager. Design stopped being just an interest and became real work. So yes — my creative journey goes all the way back to 2019. Almost seven years of history on the résumé. Not bad, right?

2024 · AGE 18
A graduation.
A new beginning.

I graduated from high school in 2024 and was accepted to Cascadia College in the United States to study computer science. That same year, I moved from Turkmenistan to Washington — a major leap into a new country, new experiences, and new possibilities.

NOW
Still learning.
Still building.

Today, I'm working on projects with Kesewi while continuing to explore the connections between graphic design, video production, and code. I'm building my own creative direction one project, experiment, and opportunity at a time.

NEXT · ?
The best part?
Still unwritten.

I don't have the whole story mapped out — and that's what makes it exciting. More ambitious ideas, new places, meaningful collaborations, and things I haven't even imagined yet. Whatever comes next, I want to keep creating and keep moving forward.
